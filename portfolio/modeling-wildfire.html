Modeling Wildfire Ocurrence

Fire risk prediction in the Paraguayan Chaco region

Among the tools to manage wildfires, accurate early prediction allows to lower the wildfire impact while diminishing logistics costs involved with fire suppression strategies (Honary et al. 2025). The objective of this project was to develop an empiric model that predicts the likelihood of a wildfire occurrence within the Paraguayan Chaco region. This can help shape responses and preparedness of response teams ahead of big fire events.
